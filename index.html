You’re here for the bison rut

Late July through August is peak bison rut in Hayden Valley and Lamar Valley — bulls bellowing, wallowing, and fighting for breeding rights. It lines up exactly with your Day 5 loop. Go early morning; the Canyon area gridlocks with bison jams from noon to dusk in August.

Wildlife spotting guide

On every trail

NPS guidance: hike in groups of 3 or more, keep the group together, carry bear spray where you can reach it in 2 seconds (not in a pack), and make noise around blind corners and streams. If a bear charges, spray at 25–40 ft aimed slightly downward.

The plan: one big shop, two top-ups

Big run Sunday Day 1 in Bozeman: gear at Sportsman’s Warehouse (Sun 10–6) FIRST, then groceries at Town & Country Foods (open til 10 PM) after — gear stores close early on Sundays, food doesn’t. Top up at Gardiner Market Day 2, restock produce/ice at Jackson Albertsons Day 7. Two coolers: one drinks-and-daily (opened constantly), one deep-storage for meats (opened once a day, stays cold).
